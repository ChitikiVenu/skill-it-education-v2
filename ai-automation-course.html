Overview

This course focuses on the practical side of AI adoption — connecting AI capability into existing workflows and tools to automate real, repetitive work.

Who Should Attend

Operations, product, or technical professionals who want to automate workflows using AI, without building AI systems from scratch.

Prerequisites
