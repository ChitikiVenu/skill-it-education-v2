Before you enroll

Questions people actually ask

Do I need a technical background to start?
No. Beginner tracks start from fundamentals. Intermediate tracks assume some prior exposure to programming or the domain — we'll help you figure out the right starting point during your free demo.
Is training in-person, online, or both?
We offer training from our Madhapur campus, with live online options for select programs. Ask your advisor which formats are currently running for your track of interest.
What do I actually walk away with?
A completed project you can show a recruiter, a course completion certificate, and interview preparation specific to the role you're targeting.
How do I get exact fees and batch dates?
Book a free demo or WhatsApp us — an advisor will share current pricing, upcoming batch dates and mode of delivery for your chosen track.
