Overview

When an incident happens, someone needs to figure out what actually occurred, in a way that holds up to scrutiny. This course covers evidence handling, timeline reconstruction, and the structured process of incident response.

Who Should Attend

SOC analysts looking to specialize, or anyone interested in the investigative side of security.

Prerequisites

Basic SOC or security fundamentals knowledge — our SOC Analyst Training is a natural prerequisite.
